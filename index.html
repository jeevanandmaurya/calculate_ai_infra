      Methodology

      Inference memory

      W = P × bw  (checkpoint bytes, shown raw)

      P = total params, bw = bytes/param (bf16 2, fp8 1, int4 0.5).

      Woverhead = W × overhead%  ·  Total = W + Woverhead + KVtot + fixed×TP

      KVtok = 2·L·k·d·bkv  (GQA/MHA)  ·  L·(r+dr)·bkv  (MLA)

      KVtot = KVtok × Savg × batch

      Inference throughput — memory-bound roofline

      tok/s = (BW × TP × η) / (Pact·bw + KVtok·Savg)

      TTFT ≈ 2·Pact·Sprompt / (TFLOP/s × TP × MFUprefill)

      commtok ≈ 4·L·h·bw·(TP−1)/TP ÷ link BW

      Training memory (per GPU, sharded by ZeRO stage)

      params = P·bp/s₃  grads = P·bg/s₂  opt = P·(bm+bv+bmaster)/s₁

      act = L·s·b·h·c  c: none = 34+5·a·s/h · selective = 34 · full = 2

      logits = s·b·V·4·2

      Training compute & time

      FLOPs = 6·Pact·D

      t = FLOPs / (N × TFLOP/s × MFU)

      Full derivations, assumptions and references: docs/calculations.md. Constants (34, 5·a·s/h, 6PD) come from Korthikanti et al. 2022 and Kaplan et al. 2020; every constant is overridable in the tuning panels.
